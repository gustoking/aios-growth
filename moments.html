Instrumentation

Measure the whole path.

Track every route. Who saw it. When they saw it. What triggered it. Whether they shared. Whether that person bought. Then compare each cohort with the current flow. The result decides the next test.

What needs tracking

Moment · day triggered · source creative · referral message · incentive shown/not shown · share · click · purchase · referred-customer quality · K30.

Operating principle

Experience should improve the questions. Not predetermine the answers.

The job is to spot the signal, understand what might be causing it, build the cleanest test and measure the result properly. Marketing responds to what the market wants — not what we want it to want.
