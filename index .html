10 / First 30 days

Get the number. Find the leak. Ship.

I wouldn't spend a month making a strategy deck. The first job is to understand the number well enough to start putting real experiments through the system.

Day 1–3

Get the number

Get access to the people and data behind K30. Reproduce 0.0835 from raw events. Map customer → referral exposure → share → click → referred customer → ≤30 days. Cut it by source, creative, cohort and time-to-referral.

Day 4–7

Find the leak

Is it exposure? Participation? Referral frequency? Click-through? Referred conversion? Timing? Acquisition mix? Customer experience? Or something none of this outside-in work has found yet?

Week 2

Ship

Take the highest-confidence, fastest-learning experiment that can materially affect K30. Not necessarily one of my prototypes.

Week 3–4

Build the loop

Results come back. Keep → kill → change → scale. Update the queue. Ship the next test. Make the learning loop faster.

If the internal data kills every idea in this presentation, good.

We've saved time and learned where not to spend engineering, design and customer attention.

By day 30: K30 is better understood, the first high-confidence experiments are running, real results are coming back, and the next tests are being decided by evidence.
