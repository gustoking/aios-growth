Objections

Answer the questions that stop the click.

Is this just medication?

No. This prototype positions Fella as a doctor-led service with clinical assessment and ongoing support. The medical team determines treatment suitability.

Will I know the cost before starting?

The live Fella site should remain the source of truth for current pricing, inclusions and terms. This concept avoids inventing live pricing.

What if I've tried lots of things before?

That is exactly the emotional state this page is designed around: not persuading someone they need to change, but showing a more supported route to starting again.

Does completing the assessment guarantee treatment?

No. Clinical eligibility and prescribing decisions belong to qualified clinicians after assessment.
